# Stage 9 — Lunar Orbit

Stage 9 is planned as the real cloud capstone: Terraform and AWS/EKS lifecycle, DNS/TLS, node scaling and failure, upgrades, Velero restore, cost awareness, and verified teardown. The required portability analysis includes GKE.

The current `stages/eks/` tree is a prototype appendix, not Stage 9 completion. Do not infer production readiness from a successful Terraform plan.
