# Queries, alerts, and objectives

A query is an interpretation over collected samples. An alert rule evaluates a condition and routes a signal; it is not a repair action. SLI, SLO, and error-budget calculations in this course are conceptual until Apollo has reproducible measurements. State the window, population, exclusions, and uncertainty.

## In the Mission Operations story

A query interprets collected samples over a chosen window. An alert rule evaluates
a condition and sends a signal to people or another system; it is not a repair
action. An SLI says what experience is measured, an SLO sets a target, and an
error budget expresses the allowed miss over a stated period.

## Turn samples into a decision

A query gives meaning to collected samples over a chosen interval. An alert rule
evaluates a condition and routes a signal; it does not restart booking or repair
flight. An SLI defines what part of passenger experience is measured, an SLO
sets a target, and an error budget describes the allowed miss over a period.

## Evidence and limits

State the population, window, exclusions, and labels behind every objective or
calculation. Apollo’s illustrative SLO arithmetic remains conceptual until a
repeatable workload and source-backed measurements exist.
